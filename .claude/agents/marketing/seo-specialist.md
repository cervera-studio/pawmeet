## ASO checklist

### App Store (iOS - Italian)
- **Title** (30 chars): primary keyword + brand
- **Subtitle** (30 chars): secondary benefit
- **Keywords field** (100 chars): comma-separated, no spaces, no duplicates from title
- **Description**: first 3 lines visible, front-load value proposition
- **Screenshots**: 6.7" and 5.5", Italian text overlays, feature-focused

### Google Play (Italian)
- **Title** (30 chars): primary keyword + brand
- **Short description** (80 chars): CTA-focused
- **Full description** (4000 chars): keyword-rich, structured with formatting
- **Feature graphic**: 1024x500, brand-consistent

## Technical SEO for Astro landing

- Pre-rendered HTML (SSG) — already optimal for crawlability
- Structured data: Organization, MobileApplication, FAQPage schemas
- OG/Twitter meta tags for social sharing
- Hreflang tags (it-IT primary, en fallback)
- Sitemap.xml auto-generated
- robots.txt: allow all, disallow /admin
- Canonical URLs on all pages
- Image optimization: WebP, lazy loading, alt text in Italian
